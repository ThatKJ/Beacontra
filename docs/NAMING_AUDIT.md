**Status:** Complete.

I have independently run a forensic collision audit on the Top 10 (+1) survivors from Phase 2. The results are highly restrictive — most of these names fail aggressively on real-world collisions in adjacent tech/AI/security spaces.

## Elimination by Severe Collision (REJECT)

*   **Provenant** — MASSIVE COLLISION. Used by a Digital Product Passport/Compliance startup, an Anti-Fraud/Authentic Communication startup (Provenant Inc.), an open-source supply chain scanner, and an AI coding agent. Way too saturated in our exact trust/compliance space.
*   **Trueshelf** — SIGNIFICANT COLLISION. TrueShelf.io is a "retailer-data intelligence provider for suppliers and brokers," which is virtually identical to our market-anomaly positioning. Also used by an AI educational startup.
*   **Corvex** — MASSIVE COLLISION. Not just a hedge fund, but Corvex (Nasdaq: MOVE) is an active AI Cloud Infrastructure public company. There is also Corvex Connected Worker (industrial IoT). Highly saturated in tech.
*   **Tracewell** — SIGNIFICANT COLLISION. "Tracewell AI" is a software startup for Agile Compliance. Also used by multiple GitHub open-source observability tools for AI (tracing LLMs). Too close to AI/compliance.
*   **Evidenza** — SIGNIFICANT COLLISION. Evidenza is a venture-backed AI-powered market research startup for synthetic research. Exact B2B market intelligence collision.
*   **Marketglass** — SIGNIFICANT COLLISION. MarketGlass™ is an executive intelligence and market research platform owned by Global Industry Analysts (GIA). Exact industry collision.
*   **Mirrorcheck** — COLLISION. MirrorCheck is a prominent research project focused on AI safety for Vision-Language Models (adversarial defense for AI). Too close to our AI/Vision focus.
*   **Echomark** — MASSIVE COLLISION. EchoMark is a well-funded ($10M) US cybersecurity startup providing forensic watermarking to protect sensitive documents/images. Exact security/evidence/image tracking space.

## Clean Survivors (KEEP)

*   **Marqline** — CLEAN. Only found an Indian private ltd and a Swiss laser-cutting business. No software or AI startup collision. Good to go.
*   **Vantle** — MOSTLY CLEAN. Used by a small voting solution and a digital studio. No major AI, security, or B2B SaaS collisions. Very safe brandable name.
*   **Glintra** — CLEAN. Only found as an Indian dermatological skincare gel and a scientific fungus name. No software/tech collisions. Safe to use.
